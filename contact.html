Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Get to know everything that can help you stay safe online

What should I do first if I get hacked?

Immediately change password on affected accounts, enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA), and disconnect compromised devices from Wi-Fi.

Is CyberSafe completely free to use?

Yes, our basic breach monitoring and scam reporting tools are 100% free for everyone.

How do I spot a phishing email or message?

Check the sender's actual email address, look for urgent demand language, and never click links promising instant money or unexpected packages.
